Who is responsible

This website is run by Manuel Mendiluce. For any privacy question, contact me through the LinkedIn link on the homepage.

What is measured

This site uses Google Analytics 4 to understand which pages are visited and how people reach them. It collects, among other things, pages viewed, approximate location (country and city, derived from the IP address), device, browser, and the site that referred you.

Measurement only starts after you press ACCEPT on the cookie notice. If you press REJECT, no analytics cookies are set and no measurement data is collected from your visit.

Cookies

  • mm_consent (necessary): remembers your choice on the cookie notice, for one year. It contains only the word granted or denied.
  • _ga and _ga_* (analytics, only after ACCEPT): used by Google Analytics to tell visits apart.

Who processes the data

Analytics data is processed by Google LLC as a service provider. Google may transfer data to the United States, under the EU–US Data Privacy Framework and Google's standard contractual clauses. More information: policies.google.com/privacy.

How long data is kept

Google Analytics keeps user-level data for a limited period (by default 2 months), after which it is deleted or aggregated. The cookies above expire as described in the table.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask for access to, correction of, or deletion of data about you, and you can withdraw your consent at any time. To withdraw consent, delete the cookies for this site in your browser: the cookie notice will show again on your next visit, and you can choose REJECT.

You also have the right to complain to the supervisory authority: in Poland, the Urząd Ochrony Danych Osobowych (UODO).
